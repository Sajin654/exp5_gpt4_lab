NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (NDA)

This Non-Disclosure Agreement is executed on 10-03-2026 by and between:
Innovate AI Labs ("Disclosing Party"), and CloudScale Systems ("Receiving Party").

PURPOSE:
The Disclosing Party agrees to share proprietary source code, machine learning model weights, and architectural designs with the Receiving Party for evaluating potential merger opportunities.

DURATION:
This Agreement shall remain in effect for a duration of 24 months from the Effective Date of 15-03-2026.

KEY OBLIGATIONS:
• Receiving Party shall hold all proprietary information in strict confidence.
• Receiving Party shall not reverse-engineer, duplicate, or distribute any proprietary assets.
• Receiving Party must notify Disclosing Party immediately upon discovery of any unauthorized access.

PAYMENT:
No payment or financial compensation is required under this agreement as it is a reciprocal confidentiality arrangement.

TERMINATION:
Either party may terminate this agreement with 15 days written notice; however, confidentiality obligations survive termination for a period of 5 years.

IMPORTANT DATES:
Effective Date: 15-03-2026
Expiration Date: 15-03-2028

POTENTIALLY IMPORTANT CLAUSES:
• Confidentiality and Non-Circumvention
• Injunction and Damages
• Governing Law under High Court of Madras
